# Comparing research

Start from a question: has Mach33's view of a specific mechanism, assumption,
or conclusion changed? Do not turn this into an exhaustive publication digest.

1. Establish the comparison baseline: dated research, a user-specified date,
   or a brief available in this conversation/pasted by the user.
   Ask for it if "last time" is otherwise undefined.
2. Read both earlier and later relevant sources. A recent summary cannot
   establish what the earlier source said.
3. Compare like with like: scope, units, currency year, forecast horizon,
   model version, scenario, and statistical convention.
4. Classify differences:
   - New reported evidence.
   - Updated assumption.
   - Changed model method or corrected error.
   - Changed conclusion.
   - Different scope, without demonstrated thesis movement.
5. Cite evidence for each asserted change. If only one side is accessible,
   say a change cannot be established from the available sources.

Suggested table:
| Earlier position (date/source) | Later position (date/source) | What changed | Why it matters |

Distinguish an explicit author revision from your inferred interpretation.
Do not infer changes from publication/update timestamps alone.
User-supplied briefs are comparison artifacts, not verified Mach33 statements;
verify their claims where possible and disclose unverifiable provenance.
